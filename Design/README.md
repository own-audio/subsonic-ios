# Icon

`AppIcon.svg` is the source of the app icon: the own.audio app-icon construction (the five
family dots around the app's symbol) in orange, Subsonic's own colour, with `</>` in the
ring's open gap at the bottom to say the app is open source. It is on the light ground the
other own.audio apps use; `AppIcon-dark.svg` is the dark-mode variant. Render each at
1024 × 1024 with no rounded corners; iOS rounds them. The welcome screen shows the same
artwork (`AppLogo` in the asset catalog, 360 px).
