A native player for the music on your own server. It works with Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, LMS, Ampache, own.audio and any other Subsonic or OpenSubsonic server.

Plays the way music should
• Gapless playback, so live and classical albums flow as recorded, or crossfade if you prefer
• Lossless FLAC and ALAC, with the format shown in the player
• Starts playing while the song is still arriving
• Volume leveling from your files' ReplayGain values
• Six-band equalizer with presets
• Synced lyrics that follow the song, where your server has them

Your whole library
• Artists, albums, playlists and search
• Recently added, recently played, most played, favorites
• Star songs, albums and artists; rate songs and albums
• Create and edit playlists
• Plays are reported to your server, which can pass them on to Last.fm or ListenBrainz

Offline
• Download albums, playlists or single songs and play them with no signal

Everywhere
• Several servers at once
• AirPlay, lock screen and Control Center
• iPhone and iPad, in English and Czech

Private by design: the app talks only to the servers you add. Your password stays in the iPhone's Keychain; only a one-time token made from it is sent. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Free and open source under the MPL-2.0. Made by own.audio.
